01About the competition

A platform for those who want to use artificial intelligence (AI) to strengthen health systems

Applicants work with participants from a range of disciplines, receive guidance from mentors and develop ideas from real health challenges. The 2027 competition is the 8th global HSIL Hackathon and the 2nd held in Thailand, organized by Mahidol University on 9–10 April 2027.

Who can apply

Students Physicians and health professionals Researchers Engineers AI and technology specialists Designers Policymakers Entrepreneurs Faculty members Anyone from a related field

Basic criteria

  1. 01

    Teams of 3–5

    Applying as a team is recommended, with members from a range of disciplines.

  2. 02

    Individuals welcome

    A team-forming activity during the event helps individual applicants join a team.

  3. 03

    No prior idea required

    An idea or prototype is not needed before applying, and a registered company is not required.

  4. 04

    Every member applies

    Every team member must apply separately, using the same team name and choosing the same Hub.

  5. 05

    Free, and the work belongs to the team

    There is no application fee, no equity is taken, and the work is the intellectual property of the team.

  6. 06

    Selection

    Selection depends on the number of places available and the suitability of each applicant.

06FAQ

Further information for applicants

Is there an application fee?

There is no application or program fee, and no equity is taken. Participants cover their own travel and accommodation during the competition in Bangkok.

What are the eligibility requirements and minimum age?

Students, physicians and health professionals, researchers, engineers, AI and technology specialists, designers, policymakers, entrepreneurs, faculty members and anyone from a related field may apply. Applicants must be 18–88 years old (Mahidol Hub criteria).

Do applicants need a team or an idea beforehand?

No. Applicants may apply individually or as a team; teams of 3–5 members from a range of disciplines are recommended. Every team member applies separately, using the same team name and choosing the same Hub. A registered company or an idea prepared in advance is not required.

Do applicants need programming skills?

No. Teams with members from a range of disciplines are recommended, and teams may present with a mock-up or prototype.

Which languages are used in the competition and the pitch?

The pitch is given in English.

Who owns the work?

The work belongs to the team, and Harvard HSIL claims no intellectual property rights. Teams must follow the Thailand Hub's intellectual property policy.

Do winning teams in Thailand go straight to Boston?

Not straight away. Selected teams may be invited to Harvard's Bootcamp, where teams are selected in rounds. Only finalist teams pitch at Demo Day in Boston; they cover their own travel, and HSIL issues letters of invitation.

Will participants receive a certificate?

Participants who attend both days receive a certificate, subject to the organizer's conditions.

How and when can applicants apply?

Applications are made through the Thailand Hub from December 2026 to mid-February 2027. Once applications open, the apply button will appear at the top of this page.
